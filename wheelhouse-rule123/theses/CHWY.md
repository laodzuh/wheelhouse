## Thesis

CHWY is a Lynch-style position: I'm a customer myself, ordering pet food
and supplies for my dog and cat through them, and the convenience of having
them deliver to my door is the entire reason I keep using them. That same
convenience is what makes the company worth holding — but I'm honest about
the moat being shallow. CHWY isn't doing anything magical; they got there
first and built scale in autoship pet supply. If a major brand (Amazon or
Walmart most plausibly) decided to match them on convenience and selection,
the differentiation would erode quickly.

I size and pace the position accordingly. The thesis is real — I use the
product, I trust the operation — but it's a thin-moat thesis, not a
Buffett-style forever holding. I wheel it with appropriately measured
conviction.
