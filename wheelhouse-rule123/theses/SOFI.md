## Thesis

SOFI is a Lynch-style position that I wheel through CSPs. I'm a customer
myself — SoFi was the easiest, fastest, and cheapest way for me to get
small loans when I needed them, and the app is genuinely good. That's the
durable bedrock: when a product is the obvious choice for me, it's likely
the obvious choice for many others, and the underlying business benefits
from that.

Unlike CHWY's thin-moat situation, SoFi has a structural advantage: the
bank charter (acquired 2022) lets it fund loans with deposit dollars
rather than wholesale capital, which is a material cost-of-funds edge.
That edge is real and durable — though credit quality is the constant
risk on the other side of any banking business.

## Current view (revisit periodically)

I expect consumer borrowing in the US to stay elevated for the next
decade or so, and SoFi is positioned to capture a meaningful share of
that demand both through direct lending and through Galileo (its B2B
platform powering other fintechs). This is a current macro view, not a
permanent thesis layer — it should be revisited periodically as the
borrowing environment evolves.

## Watch conditions

The bot watches for these on my behalf and pings me when any fires; I
don't track them manually.

- **Credit-quality deterioration.** Spikes in SoFi's charge-off rate
  (loans written off as uncollectible) or its default rate (loans
  going non-current and likely to default). For a bank, credit quality
  is the single biggest risk vector — when bad-loan rates ramp fast,
  earnings get hit through provisions for credit losses, and persistent
  spikes signal systemic underwriting problems. *(Bot tracks both
  metrics from quarterly filings.)*
- **Personal canary.** I stop using SoFi for my own borrowing or banking
  in a meaningful way — switching to a different lender, finding the
  product no longer meeting my needs, etc. Same logic as CHWY: the
  insight that drove me to invest is the same insight that should
  signal when conviction's gone. *(Bot can't track directly; flags a
  quarterly check-in for me to confirm.)*

When either fires, the bot's job is to surface the trigger and prompt
a thesis review — not to recommend a sell. The decision stays mine.
